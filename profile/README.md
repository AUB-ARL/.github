# Aerial Robotics Lab (ARL)

**American University of Beirut**

The Aerial Robotics Lab conducts research in aerial robotics, autonomous systems, perception, planning, guidance, navigation, and control.

## Research Areas

- Aerial Robotics
- Autonomous Systems
- Perception and Computer Vision
- Guidance, Navigation, and Control
- Planning and Decision Making
- Multi-Robot Systems

## Research Code

This organization hosts software, simulation tools, experimental code, datasets, and documentation developed within the Aerial Robotics Lab.

## Collaboration

ARL brings together students and researchers working on robotics and autonomous systems, with an emphasis on reproducible research and collaborative development.
